Recycled Art - Most paintings become castaways. The jetsam floats to transfer stations, yard sales, thrift stores and dumpsters.
I salvage the wrecks for resurrection as gaff rigged Schooners, Sloops and Ketches.
A weathered, cedar fence becomes a hull. Sun flower stalks work well as booms.
Upcycled materials find new value and appreciation.
